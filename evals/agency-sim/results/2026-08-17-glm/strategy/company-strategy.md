# Mise — Company Strategy

**Mission.** Mise is an AI-native web-design agency that ships beautiful, fast, brand-aligned websites for small food & hospitality businesses in a fraction of the time and cost of a traditional agency.

**Target market.** Independent cooking brands, restaurants, bakeries, and food creators who need a real website with a blog but are priced out of traditional agencies ($5k–$30k). Our sweet-spot client has $0 revenue online and wants a launch-ready site in days, not months.

**Service offering.**
- *Launch Site* — up to 5 static pages + blog, brand-tuned, mobile-first. Fixed scope, fixed price.
- *Content Engine* — recurring blog post packs (recipes, stories) to drive organic traffic.
- *Brand Identity Sprint* — name, palette, type, logo direction in one deliverable.

**Pricing sketch.** Transparent fixed-price packages. *Launch Site* $1,500 flat (vs. $3k–$8k agency norm). *Brand Identity Sprint* $600 add-on. *Content Engine* $250/month per 4-post batch. No hourly billing — predictability is the product.

**Competitive wedge.** AI agents do the drafting/coding work; a human founder curates and approves. Result: agency-quality output at freelance pricing, delivered in days.

**Org design (C-suite + ICs).**
- **CTO** — owns the technical build, site architecture, code quality. Critical for credibility.
- **Head of Design** — owns visual brand, layout, palette/typography for both agency and client.
- **CMO** — owns marketing collateral, social, outreach, positioning.
- **Frontend Engineer (IC)** — writes the actual HTML/CSS. Needs strongest code model.
- **Content Writer (IC)** — writes real recipe/food blog content with culinary credibility.

Five hires keeps overhead lean while covering build, design, brand, and marketing.
