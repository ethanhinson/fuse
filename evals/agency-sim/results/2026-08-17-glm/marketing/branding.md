# Marketing/Branding

## A) AGENCY BRAND — "Mise"

**Name Meaning**: From French *mise* ("to place"), reflecting intentional design and spatial harmony.

**Tagline**: "Crafting Design, Elevating Brands"

**Color Palette**: 
- Navy Blue (#2A3B5A): Trustworthy foundation
- Soft Gray (#F0F4F8): Modern contrast
- Muted Sage (#6C757D): Subtle accents
- Warm Amber (#E9C46A): Creative energy

**Typography**: 
- Headings: Montserrat SemiBold (clean, modern)
- Body: Quicksand Regular (readable, approachable)

**Logo Direction**: Minimalist sans-serif mark with a stylized "M" incorporating a subtle chef’s hat silhouette in navy, accented with amber.

## B) CLIENT COOKING BRAND — "Forge & Hearth"

**Tagline**: "Fresh Flavors, Simple Meals — Baked with Care"

**Color Palette**: 
- Terracotta (#D39E6A): Warmth for hero sections and callouts
- Soft Cream (#F8F4E3): Light backgrounds and card bases
- Deep Navy (#2D3436): Bold headers and accents
- Vibrant Orange (#FF6B35): Interactive elements and highlights

**Typography**: 
- Headings: Montserrat Bold (strong, artisanal feel)
- Body: Quicksand Regular (friendly, approachable)

**Logo Direction**: Hand-drawn flourishes around the brand name, blending a forge anvil and hearth flame in terracotta/orange, set against navy.

**Layout Direction**: Hero section with full-width imagery, stacked recipe cards with soft cream backgrounds, and ample white space. Vertical spacing follows 1.5x body font size; horizontal spacing uses 2rem gutters. All elements aligned to the left for a clean, food-forward aesthetic.
