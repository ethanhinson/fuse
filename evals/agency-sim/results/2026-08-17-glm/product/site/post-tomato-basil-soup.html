Roasted Tomato Basil Soup

A hearty, comforting soup with fresh tomatoes and basil.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs cherry tomatoes
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 4 cups vegetable broth
  • 1/2 cup chopped basil
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Toss tomatoes with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Roast for 25-30 minutes.
  2. In a large pot, sauté onion and garlic until softened. Add roasted tomatoes and broth. Simmer for 15 minutes.
  3. Puree the mixture until smooth. Stir in basil and season to taste. Serve warm.
