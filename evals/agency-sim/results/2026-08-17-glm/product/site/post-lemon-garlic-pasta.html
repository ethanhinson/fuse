Weeknight Lemon Garlic Pasta

A quick and easy weeknight meal that’s bursting with flavor.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz spaghetti
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 lemon (juiced)
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley

Method

  1. Cook pasta according to package instructions until al dente. Drain and set aside.
  2. In a large pan, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add garlic and sauté until fragrant.
  3. Add lemon juice, salt, and pepper. Cook for 1-2 minutes.
  4. Toss the cooked pasta into the pan. Mix well to coat.
  5. Stir in Parmesan and parsley. Serve immediately.
