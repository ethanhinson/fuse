Sheet-Pan Lemon Herb Chicken with Veggies

A one-pan meal with juicy chicken and colorful vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs chicken breasts
  • 1.5 lbs mixed vegetables (carrots, zucchini, bell peppers)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon (zest and juice)
  • 1 tbsp dried herbs (thyme, rosemary, oregano)
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Method

  1. Season chicken and veggies with salt, pepper, herbs, and lemon zest. Toss with olive oil.
  2. Spread on a parchment-lined sheet pan. Roast at 425°F for 25-30 minutes, flipping halfway.
  3. Let rest for 5 minutes before serving. Enjoy with a side of crusty bread.
